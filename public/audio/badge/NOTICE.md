# Badge interaction audio

The two audio samples distributed with this project are adapted from real object
recordings on Freesound, licensed under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

| File | Recording and author | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| plastic-flip-audible.wav | Plastic Cards (credit, debit, etc).wav by afleetingspeck | https://freesound.org/people/afleetingspeck/sounds/151177/ |
| buckle-soft.wav | Belt Buckle by IndigoRay | https://freesound.org/people/IndigoRay/sounds/331719/ |

Adapted from the high-quality MP3 previews provided by Freesound. Edits include
short excerpts, high/low-pass filtering, edge fades, level adjustments, and mono
44.1 kHz PCM WAV conversion. The plastic-card sample also uses dynamic
compression to make its brief contact audible at low playback volume.

Playback gains: card flip 0.40 × 2/3 × 1/2; metal contact on release and character
switch 0.42. Character-switch audio follows the new card's first visible catch
and reversal. Rope pull and recoil sounds are disabled; earlier local audition
assets are not distributed. These are adapted foley, not recordings of the exact
pictured badge. Character switching uses the same metal sample as release.
