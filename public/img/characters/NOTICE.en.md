## Original character artwork

Eight character illustrations come from [ZipZipPipe’s original post](https://www.bilibili.com/opus/1233069202138464289). Downloaded originals retain their original bytes; files were renamed and are cropped or scaled for display. The site does not use the post’s small grid thumbnails.

- DeepSeek Whale-chan: original character by 上善无形 (Shangshan Wuxing), maid redesign by ZipZipPipe. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- The main DeepSeek badge uses the previously archived transparent `../deepseek/maid.webp`. Its Small-tailqwq / JAdpp adaptation credits, source links and hashes are retained in the [original asset notes](../deepseek/NOTICE.md). The gallery uses the original-post PNG.
- GPT, Kimi, GLM, Qwen, Gemini, Claude and Grok: ZipZipPipe. Used with attribution for non-commercial fan display under the artist’s sharing statement. Third-party character rights are not claimed by this project, and these images are not relicensed under MIT.
- Character descriptions, departments and employee numbers are creative additions for this display, not official character lore or product capability claims.
- The second illustration in the original post is Kimi; the seventh is GPT Dragon-chan.

Per-file source URLs, original dimensions and SHA-256 hashes: [sources.json](./sources.json).

## Chibi portraits

The nine transparent portraits in `../chibi/` were generated with image_gen from this project's previews; they are not the original artist's chibi files. GPT, GLM, Qwen, Gemini, Claude, Kimi and Grok follow ZipZipPipe character designs referenced through video stills in the [ai-girls archive](https://github.com/shuaizenge/ai-girls); the eyes of GPT, Claude and Grok were adjusted with the stills as reference, and Kimi uses the user's chosen version. DeepSeek and Doubao follow user-provided references; DeepSeek retains credits to Shangshan Wuxing and ZipZipPipe. Character and brand rights remain with their respective owners, and these illustrations are not licensed under the code's MIT license. DeepSeek's design retains attribution, non-commercial and share-alike requirements.

## Doubao

The current v4 illustration is AI-generated anime fan art, with short brown hair, a red scarf, a black blazer and skirt, a waist chain, tall boots and a handbag. It is not an original Doubao illustration by ZipZipPipe.

- Displayed file: `doubao-anime-v4.png`, 941 × 1672, transparent RGBA, generated using image_gen.
- Appearance follows the user-provided reference used for v3; the existing DeepSeek artwork provided the visual style reference. Reference credits to 上善无形 / ZipZipPipe are retained.
