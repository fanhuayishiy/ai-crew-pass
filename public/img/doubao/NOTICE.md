# doubao layered fan artwork v1

AI-generated unofficial fan character, based on the user reference and the existing Doubao anime design.

Three separate image_gen outputs: portrait-cutout-v1.png (true alpha), front-background-v1.png and back-background-v1.png. WebP siblings are export sources. Text and layout are composed in Astro. These fan derivatives are not official brand artwork and are not automatically MIT-licensed. Full prompts: docs/remaining-layered-cards-v1.md in the repository.
